# Website Design for Architects

Websites for architecture firms in India with project archives, photography, detailed case study pages, and a CMS your team can update.

I design and build websites for architecture practices in India. Give prospective clients a way to explore your built work, understand your approach, and get in touch about a brief.

## A portfolio organised around your work

I plan the project archive around how clients browse your practice, such as by building type, location, or stage. Each project can have its own page with a brief, photography, drawings, and credits. A residential client can find relevant projects without going through the whole archive.

## Room for drawings and photography

Layouts can pair wide photographs with elevations, detail shots, and captions. I use responsive image sizes so a phone can load a smaller file while a desktop can show more detail. The project story remains readable alongside the gallery.

## A publishing workflow for your team

With a CMS, your team can create a project page, choose a cover image, reorder the gallery, and add credits. I shape the editing fields around what you already document for each project and review the publishing workflow at handover.

## What should an architecture project page include?

Start with the project type, location, and a short description of the brief. Show the finished work alongside any plans or process images that explain an important design decision. Include photographer and collaborator credits, and check which client and site details are approved for publication.

## Can we launch with a small portfolio?

Yes. A studio profile, a short selection of work, and a clear enquiry form can fit on one page. A project archive and CMS are helpful when you have enough work to organise by type or want to publish updates regularly.

## Can you rebuild an existing architecture website?

Yes. I start by reviewing the pages, project photography, and existing URLs. We decide what to carry over and map redirects for pages that move so visitors with old links can still find the work.
